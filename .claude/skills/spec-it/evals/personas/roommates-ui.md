2. Split types are equal and exact amount only.
3. The app records paybacks; no payments are processed.
4. Rounding remainder rotates between members instead of always going to the payer.
5. A member cannot leave a household while their balance is not zero.
6. Settled expenses are locked; unsettled ones are editable by anyone in the household.
7. If an expense changes while someone is settling up, they see the new amount before confirming.
8. One email when someone records a payback to you; no other notifications.
9. Joining is by invite link, no passwords.
10. Recurring expenses (rent) are out of scope for v1.
